**OpenVK is a universal tool for finding people you know, based on the VKontakte structure.**

We want friends, fellow students, former classmates, neighbors, and colleagues to always be able to stay in touch.

With this app, you can:

- Find people you have studied, worked, or spent time with in the past.
- Learn more about the people around you and make new friends.
- Stay in touch with those who matter to you.
- Promote your creative work and/or share your views.
